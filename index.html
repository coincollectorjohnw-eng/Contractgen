How to write a contract 
Put any blank in [square brackets]:
  [Employee Full Legal Name]
Each different bracket becomes a form field.
Use the same bracket twice and it fills in both places.
[Day] [Month] [Year] auto-fill with today's date.
[Date] auto-fills with the full date.
